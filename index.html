The work of Aaron Nichols, the Digital Blacksmith.

I continue a family craft forged in the Scottish Highlands. My ancestors were blacksmiths who shaped iron into tools that served the practical demands of their age. That same discipline of precision, refinement, and purposeful construction now guides the work of this forge.

Here I produce news analysis, video presentations, and structured reporting designed to clarify complex geopolitical, historical, and strategic developments. The medium has changed from iron to information and imagery, yet the purpose remains constant: to produce clear, durable instruments of understanding.

This is the forge.
